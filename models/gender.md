# face_gender

Gender classification — male / female.

## Plus (CNN)

- Test accuracy: 88.6% (UTKFace)
- Real-world accuracy: 80–85%
- Parameters: 3.3M
- Size: ~38 MB

Accuracy is limited by hardware constraints (8GB VRAM, 16GB RAM). Optimizations are in progress.

## Status

CNN models for Nano and Baseline are in training. They will be published when ready.
